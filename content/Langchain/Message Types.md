**Message Types** in LangChain are standardized classes used for structuring conversations with LLMs, especially in chat-based applications:

1. **SystemMessage**: Sets the context or role at the start of a chat (e.g., “You are a helpful assistant”).
2. **HumanMessage**: Messages typed by the user to the LLM.
3. **AIMessage**: Responses generated by the LLM.  
    These types help maintain clarity about who says what, essential for managing conversation flow and context in multi-turn dialogues.
