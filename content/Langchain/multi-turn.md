**Multi-turn Conversations** maintain a conversation history by sending a list of messages (using the message types) back and forth with the LLM, allowing context retention across exchanges. Multi-turn interactions are typical in chatbots, where previous user inputs and AI responses form context. LangChain supports this through the **ChatPromptTemplate** class and uses a [[MessagePlaceholder]] to dynamically insert entire chat histories into prompts, enabling the model to understand continuity and provide coherent answers reflecting prior conversation.

In summary, PromptTemplate enables creating structured and dynamic prompts for LLMs with consistency and validation; message types classify and organize chat conversation parts in LangChain; single-turn is for isolated interactions; and multi-turn leverages message history for continuous, context-aware dialogue. These concepts together empower building sophisticated LLM-powered applications like research assistants and chatbots.
