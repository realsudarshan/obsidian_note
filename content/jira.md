Scrum vs Kanban
- Used in [[agile development]] widely.Three roles:product owner,dev team and customers/users
- [[User stories]] are wriiten in [[product backlog]]
- Scrum and Kanban are [[pull system]]
- Scrum teams work in series of [[sprints]]
- In each starting of new sprint,the item of product backlog is pulled to [[sprint backlog]] which are priority and developer ensure to finish in time
- Any new task had to wait for next sprint
- The choosen task are kept in scrum board or kanban board and moved to different checkpoint/column like todo,build,test,done
- Incomplete task go back to product backlog

In kanban,
It is continous and task can be added anytime
But there is limit and limited task can be  in a column
If any column is empty,previous task is moved to it.
